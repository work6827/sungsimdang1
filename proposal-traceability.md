## Proposed Improvements and Trade-offs
1. Use conventional state and QR tokens for the MVP while retaining blockchain credentials as a future concept. A local stamp is not a blockchain asset.
2. Compare manual/POS/phone workflows before purchasing glasses. Simulate recognition without claiming accuracy.
3. Use one slot and a 30-minute pickup window for a repeatable classroom demo; these are not verified business rules.
4. Separate the two-week practicum from the 60-day return panel; actual retention cannot be measured in two weeks.
5. Make confidence a labeled sensitivity band until calibrated data exists. Do not show forecast accuracy without a held-out test.
