# Staff AI glasses and inventory

## What works in this MVP
Operations Center provides a store/product selector, an illustrated shelf scene, a “Simulate shelf scan” button, predefined bounding overlays, a visible count and illustrative confidence. Staff can correct the visible count and separately enter confirmed backroom stock. Submitting the form creates a timestamped total-stock snapshot. Forecasts, kitchen advice and assistant answers read that same state.

The visible count is generated by a fixed fixture rule, not image recognition. The confidence is a display fixture, not tested accuracy. No camera is opened; no images, faces or audio are collected. Original SVG illustrations are placeholders, not photographs of actual inventory.

## Why visible count is not inventory
Overlapping products, similar shapes, crowded trays, people in view, lighting, perspective and packaging can produce incorrect counts. A shelf image says nothing about unseen storage or kitchen work in progress. The demo requires a separate backroom confirmation. Last-observed shelf/backroom fields remain historical after simulated consumption; the current snapshot and timestamp are displayed separately.

## Proposed real workflow
Permissioned camera image → approved on-device or secure inference → product detections → uncertainty/occlusion checks → staff correction → reconciliation with POS, kitchen and waste records → confirmed stock update. Keep confidence and observation time with every record. Low confidence should request manual review rather than overwrite trusted stock.

Potential hardware includes enterprise smart glasses, consumer camera glasses, a phone or fixed shelf camera. Compare staff comfort, battery, field of view, hygiene, accessibility, SDK/data access, support and total ownership cost. A wearable is not automatically better than manual/POS updates.

## Proposed safeguards, not implemented compliance
Use shelf-only framing, capture indicators, staff authorization, no audio by default, minimal capture, short retention, restricted access and audit logs. Avoid faces, receipts, payment screens and employee tracking. Prefer on-device processing and discard raw imagery where feasible. Consult staff and privacy reviewers before a real pilot. Include a non-camera manual alternative. Local MVP pages are not authenticated staff systems.

## Evaluation plan
Use permissioned labeled shelf images or staged trays with known counts. Test overlap, lighting, similar products and partial obstruction separately. Measure absolute count error, correction rate, correction time and failure detection. Interview staff about workload, safety and privacy acceptance. Do not compute “accuracy” from our predefined overlays; that would only compare a fixture with itself.
